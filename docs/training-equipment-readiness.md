# Pilot Training equipment readiness

Training consumes the shared offline Review and accepted fitting identity/date/
fingerprint. Equipment is VERIFIED, NOT_READY or UNKNOWN from fresh authoritative
observation. Evidence expires after 60 seconds; refresh recomputes it and changed
fit/hull/control/completeness cannot retain green readiness.

DUTY READY = existing Skills READY + Equipment VERIFIED. Supply FULL/LOW/MISSING/
UNKNOWN remains independent; LOW alone does not clear generic Duty Ready.
Existing skill thresholds, funding, purchase, onboarding, queue and Home policy
are unchanged.

Provision Equipment is manual and shows an accepted plan. Personal local stock
or an exact corporation/division is separate from the definition provider. The
shared Center free-only lifecycle revalidates Training policy after acquisition,
then invokes the same hull/custody engine and releases safely. There is no
Training loader, matcher, custody journal or automatic equip side effect.
Busy/recovery and CEO temporary acquisition refuse; no corporation fallback.

Accepted evidence covers exact equipment/Duty Ready, one real manual provisioning
path, LOW supplies, busy refusal and freshness. Skills NOT READY with verified
equipment (T3) and selected fitting-definition drift are deterministic
production-path proofs, not claimed as exact live cases. Publication needs only
a read-only exact equipment + LOW display smoke when engine semantics are unchanged.
